Welcome home

A church where the Spirit still moves

Our services are full of heartfelt worship, prayer at the altar, and anointed preaching from the Word of God. We believe the same Holy Ghost poured out in the Book of Acts is still being poured out today.

Whether you grew up in an apostolic church or you've never set foot in one, we'd be glad to have you. We'll save you a seat.

What we believe

Built on the apostles' doctrine

Our teaching follows Acts 2:42: "And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers."

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Colossians 2:9

The Oneness of God

There is one God, and He was manifest in the flesh as Jesus Christ. In Him dwells all the fullness of the Godhead bodily.

Acts 3:19 · Luke 13:3

Repentance

Salvation begins with turning away from sin and turning toward God with a sincere heart.

Acts 2:38 · Acts 4:12

Baptism in Jesus' Name

Water baptism by full immersion in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ for the remission of sins.

Acts 2:4 · Acts 10:46

The Holy Ghost

Every believer can be filled with the Holy Ghost, with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues.

Hebrews 12:14 · 1 Peter 1:16

Holiness

A life set apart for God, in heart, conduct, and appearance, as a daily expression of love for Him.

2 Timothy 3:16

The Word of God

The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and living.
